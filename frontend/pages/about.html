OUR VISION

About CraveSpot

CraveSpot is built on a simple yet powerful human truth: When hunger strikes, you don't always think of a restaurant brand — you think of a craving.

Whether you're yearning for "something spicy and crispy under ₹200", a warm homestyle North Indian curry on a rainy evening, or a midnight cheesy pizza slice, CraveSpot connects your immediate desires to local chefs, vendors, and food artisans.

Why CraveSpot is Different

Unlike conventional delivery aggregators that push sponsored listings, CraveSpot puts craving-based discovery first. We prioritize:

  • Taste & Texture Matching: Finding foods by flavor profiles (spicy, cheesy, sweet, creamy, crispy, tangy).
  • Real Geolocation Intelligence: Measuring true walking and driving distances to verified restaurants.
  • Budget Transparency: Helping students and foodies find satisfying meals within their exact pocket limits.
