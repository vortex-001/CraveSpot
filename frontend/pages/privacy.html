Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At CraveSpot, accessible at CraveSpot, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by CraveSpot and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use CraveSpot, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Data: Name, email address, contact number, and encrypted passwords when you register.
  • Location Information: Geolocation coordinates (latitude and longitude) provided with your explicit browser permission to discover nearby food and compute walking/driving distances.
  • Craving & Search Queries: Terms and taste preferences entered into search bars to improve recommendations.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to deliver personalized craving discovery, estimate vendor distances, save dining preferences, and ensure application security.

3. Security

User passwords are encrypted using state-of-the-art cryptographic hashing (Werkzeug scrypt). We do not sell or monetize personal user data.
